Contributor Guidelines

Renewal News Network welcomes original submissions that align with its editorial principles. Contributors should be aware that publication is selective and editorial discretion is final.

What We Publish

  • Analytical essays on governance, law, and public policy
  • Well-argued commentary grounded in evidence
  • Public-interest writing of lasting relevance

What We Do Not Publish

  • Personal attacks or defamatory material
  • Unsubstantiated allegations
  • Purely promotional or partisan content
  • Material generated by automated systems without disclosure

Submission Standards

  • Clear thesis and logical structure
  • Sources cited where appropriate
  • Original work not published elsewhere
  • Approximate length: 800–2,500 words

Review Process

Submissions are reviewed for relevance, clarity, and adherence to editorial standards. Acceptance, revision, or rejection decisions are communicated at the editors’ discretion.

Rights

By submitting work, contributors grant Renewal News Network the right to publish and archive the material. Authors retain moral authorship unless otherwise agreed.

How to Submit

Submissions and enquiries should be sent via the contact form on the website. Unsolicited submissions may not receive individual feedback.
